# Complementary & Natural Medicine for Crohn's Disease — Index

Per-topic reference files split from the monolithic `13-complementary-medicine.md`. One file per intervention for grep-friendly lookup and per-topic maintenance.

**Framing**: "What does the evidence actually say?" — no boosterism, no dismissiveness.

**Evidence grades**: `[Strong]` = multiple well-powered RCTs; `[Moderate]` = at least one positive RCT or consistent supporting data; `[Weak]` = small/inconsistent trials or negative RCTs; `[Anecdotal]` = case reports / preclinical only.

**Note**: Most IBD complementary evidence is for ulcerative colitis (UC). CD-specific evidence is thinner — flag this when extrapolating.

**This document is a reference aid, not medical advice.** Crohn's disease management requires individualized care with a qualified gastroenterologist. Most "natural" interventions are adjunctive — they work alongside, not instead of, evidence-based medical therapy when disease is active. The most dangerous supplement is the one that delays effective treatment while inflammation silently damages the bowel.
