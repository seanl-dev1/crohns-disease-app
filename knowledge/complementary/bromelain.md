# Bromelain

## SUPPLEMENT: bromelain

### Evidence Grade
**[Anecdotal/Preclinical]** — promising mechanistic and animal data; no IBD RCT in humans.

### Mechanism
- Proteolytic enzyme mixture from pineapple stem/fruit (*Ananas comosus*)
- Systemic anti-inflammatory when taken on empty stomach (gets absorbed somewhat intact)
- Reduces pro-inflammatory cytokines (TNF-α, IL-1β, IL-6) — demonstrated in colon biopsies ex vivo
- **Cleaves TNF receptors**, reducing signaling
- Reduces leukocyte migration, thrombin generation
- Fibrinolytic activity

### Evidence
- **Hale LP, et al. 2005** (PMID: 15936249): Oral bromelain reduced colitis in IL-10 knockout mice
- **Hale LP, et al. 2010** (PMID: 20848493): Fresh pineapple juice reduced colonic neoplasia in IL-10 KO mice with colitis
- **Onken JE, et al. 2008**: Bromelain reduced cytokine secretion from UC/CD colon biopsies ex vivo
- **Kane S, Goldberg MJ. 2000** (PMID: 10766699): Case report — 2 UC patients in remission on bromelain after failing conventional therapy (uncontrolled)
- **Marzoli F, et al. 2025** (PMC12663318): RCT in UC (triple-blind, placebo-controlled) — some evidence of benefit. **Need to verify PMID and primary results — this was a recent trial.**
- **No CD RCT.**

### Dose
- 200-400 mg 2-3x/day on empty stomach (for systemic anti-inflammatory effect); 500 mg is typical
- Strength measured in **GDU (gelatin dissolving units)** or **MCU (milk clotting units)** — 2,000-2,400 GDU/g is standard
- With meals for digestive enzyme effect (not anti-inflammatory)

### Safety
- Generally well tolerated
- GI upset, nausea, diarrhea
- **Rare allergic reactions** — especially in those with pineapple, latex, bee sting, or pollen allergies (cross-reactivity)
- Increased bleeding risk (antiplatelet) — caution with anticoagulants, preoperative

### Drug Interactions
- **Antibiotics** — may increase absorption of amoxicillin, tetracyclines
- **Anticoagulants/antiplatelets** — bleeding risk
- **Sedatives** — possible additive effect (minor)

### Practical Bottom Line
Mechanistically interesting, human IBD evidence scant. Low cost, generally safe. If tried, 500 mg TID on empty stomach. Don't expect miracles. **Stop before surgery.** May pair logically with curcumin for multi-target anti-inflammation (but stack bleeding risk).
