# Exclusive Enteral Nutrition (EEN)

## DIET: een

### Evidence Grade
**[Strong]** in **pediatric CD** induction (equivalent to steroids for clinical remission, superior for mucosal healing, no side effects). **[Moderate-Weak]** in adult CD.

### Protocol
- **EEN**: 100% of caloric needs from polymeric or elemental formula (no other food) × **6-8 weeks**
- Polymeric (whole protein, e.g., Modulen IBD, Ensure, Peptamen) as effective as elemental (amino acid) in most trials
- Oral or NG tube delivery
- Followed by gradual food reintroduction

### Pediatric Evidence
- **Borrelli O, et al. 2006**: EEN superior to corticosteroids for mucosal healing
- **Meta-analysis Zachos M, et al. 2007** (Cochrane): steroids slightly superior for clinical remission BUT EEN superior for growth and mucosal healing, no steroid side effects
- **Narula N, et al. 2018** (Cochrane, PMID: 28815649): pediatric meta-analysis confirming EEN efficacy
- **ESPGHAN/ECCO 2020 guidelines**: EEN is **first-line induction therapy for pediatric mild-moderate CD**
- Modified EEN (MEN) and CDED + PEN (partial EEN) — better tolerated, similar efficacy (Levine — below)

### Adult Evidence
- Generally inferior adherence due to palatability
- Meta-analyses (González-Huix, Zachos): steroids modestly superior in adults
- **Still considered reasonable** for patients wanting to avoid steroids, preoperative optimization, stricturing disease

### Mechanism
- Removes dietary antigens (gluten, emulsifiers, FODMAPs, food additives)
- Alters microbiome (reduces pro-inflammatory taxa)
- Restores mucosal barrier
- Nutritional repletion

### Dose/Calories
- Total caloric needs (e.g., 25-35 kcal/kg/day)
- Split into 4-6 portions daily
- Polymeric formulas typically 1-1.5 kcal/mL
- 6-8 week duration minimum

### Safety
- Very safe
- Short-term: refeeding syndrome (electrolyte monitoring in malnourished), mild nausea/diarrhea on initiation
- Social/psychological burden (eating restriction)
- Micronutrient considerations if prolonged

### Practical Bottom Line
**Gold standard for pediatric CD induction.** For adults: underused, worth considering for steroid-sparing induction, preoperative optimization, strictures with inflammatory component. Requires high motivation and dietitian support. **Preoperative EEN** for 4-6 weeks before surgery reduces complications (PMID: 31880668 and others).
