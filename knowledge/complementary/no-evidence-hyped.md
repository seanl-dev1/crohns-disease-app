# No-Evidence / Often-Hyped Interventions

## SAFETY: no-evidence

### Colloidal Silver
- **No benefit in IBD.** Marketed for "immune support" and various infections.
- FDA banned OTC use in 1999 due to misbranding/lack of safety evidence
- **Argyria** — irreversible blue-gray skin/mucosa/organ discoloration from silver deposition
- Impairs absorption of antibiotics, thyroxine
- Can cause kidney, liver, nervous system damage
- **Avoid completely.** No legitimate oral use.

### Miracle Mineral Solution (MMS) / Chlorine Dioxide
- Sodium chlorite solution activated with acid → **chlorine dioxide, an industrial bleach**
- Promoted for autism, cancer, HIV, COVID-19, IBD — **all completely unfounded**
- FDA warnings since 2010; promoters (Genesis II Church) criminally prosecuted
- Causes severe vomiting, dehydration, life-threatening hypotension, methemoglobinemia, hemolysis (G6PD deficiency), bowel damage
- **Drinking this is drinking bleach. Extremely dangerous.**

### "Parasite Cleanses" / Detox Programs
- Most commercial "detox" products: no established mechanism, no quality trials, often contain laxatives/diuretics
- **Worsen** IBD through dehydration, electrolyte loss, bowel irritation
- "Parasites" implicated in IBD pathogenesis is fringe — **helminth therapy** (TSO, *Trichuris suis* ova) briefly studied and mostly abandoned after negative RCTs (Summers 2005 positive pilot not replicated)

### Homeopathy
- No evidence beyond placebo in IBD or anywhere else
- Mechanism implausible (dilution beyond Avogadro's number)
- Time/money sink; unlikely to cause harm itself but delays effective treatment

### Ozone Therapy / Hyperbaric Oxygen
- HBO has some signal in **fistulizing CD** (small uncontrolled series) — not ready for prime time
- Ozone insufflation: unregulated, risk of oxidative damage, no IBD evidence

### Coffee Enemas / Gerson Therapy
- No evidence
- Documented harm: colitis, perforation, electrolyte imbalance, death
- Avoid

### "Organic Germanium" / Ge-132
- Liver and kidney toxicity case reports
- No IBD evidence
- Avoid

### Black Seed Oil (Nigella sativa)
- Preclinical anti-inflammatory signal
- No IBD RCTs; promoted in health-food spaces without evidence
- Generally safe but don't expect benefit

### Kombucha
- No IBD-specific evidence
- Contains live microbes (variable); caution in immunosuppressed patients — rare infections reported
- Acidic — may aggravate reflux
- Low harm generally; not a treatment

### Apple Cider Vinegar
- No IBD evidence
- Very acidic — can aggravate reflux, erode tooth enamel
- Trendy, unsupported

### Activated Charcoal
- Adsorbs some toxins but also medications and nutrients
- **Will absorb oral IBD medications** — avoid concurrent use
- No IBD treatment evidence
