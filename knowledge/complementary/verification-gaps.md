# Gaps I Couldn't Verify

## META: verification-gaps

These are items where I could not fully verify the PMID, primary data, or specific attribution from my research sources. The claims exist in secondary literature but I want to flag them rather than assert:

1. **Shoba 1998 piperine bioavailability paper** — PMID 9619120 claimed in sources but the exact bioavailability figure (2000%) needs original-paper verification; figure varies in citations (some say 20-fold, some 2000%). (Same thing numerically — 2000% = 20×, but worth confirming the primary number.)

2. **Gupta I et al. 2001 Boswellia trial for colitis** — referenced in review articles but specific PMID and dose details were harder to pin down than Gerhardt 2001. PMID 9049593 for a 1997 Gupta paper on chronic colitis exists but the 2001 paper dose details less clear.

3. **Cramer H 2017 yoga in UC RCT** — I'm confident it's *Aliment Pharmacol Ther* 2017 45(11):1379-89, but didn't explicitly confirm PMID 28378342 via PubMed search. Secondary analysis by Evers 2019 (*J Psychosom Res*) confirmed effects.

4. **Keefer L et al. 2013 GDH for UC maintenance** — I'm confident this exists in Journal of Clinical Gastroenterology 2013 (sometimes cited as Keefer 2013 gut-directed hypnotherapy), but specific PMID I couldn't lock down with certainty.

5. **Mawdsley JE et al. 2008 hypnosis reduces mucosal inflammation in UC** — PMID referenced as 18637160 from memory; specific paper in *Gut* 2008 needs confirmation.

6. **Konijeti GG 2017 AIP pilot** — referenced in IBD literature; specific PMID I couldn't confirm. *Inflamm Bowel Dis* 2017 23(11):2054-2060 sounds right, but unverified.

7. **Ananthakrishnan AN 2012 vitamin D + IBD surgery** — PMID unverified; review cites this work.

8. **Jedel S 2014 MBSR prevented UC flares** — cited in reviews (PMID 24820332 from memory); not directly fetched.

9. **Sinniger V 2020 VNS CD follow-up** — *Neurogastroenterol Motil* citation unverified.

10. **Bourreille A 2013 S. boulardii negative CD trial** — I have the PMID 23466709, but double-checking recommended.

11. **Naftali T 2021 oral CBD-rich CD RCT** — I have PMID 33858011 but the 34137450 number mentioned in my search results may conflict; both candidates should be cross-checked.

12. **Lewis JD 2021 DINE-CD PMID 34052278** — solid; verified through multiple sources.

13. **Sugimoto K 2020 Theracurmin CD PMID 32412598** — verified.

14. **Belluzzi A 1996 NEJM fish oil in CD** — I referenced this but didn't directly fetch; commonly cited as contrast with EPIC.

15. **Strazzabosco et al. 2025 Mediterranean diet CD multiomic** — I know of the *Gastroenterology* 2025 paper from search results; haven't verified author name spelling precisely.

16. **Marzoli F et al. 2025 bromelain UC RCT** — I saw reference to a 2025 publication (PMC12663318) but didn't fully verify author and primary findings.

17. **Langmead 2002 herbal combo UC trial** — older uncontrolled data, PMID not confirmed.

18. **Mahmood A 2007 zinc carnosine Gut paper** — likely correct citation but PMID not verified.

19. **Specific numerical remission rates for various pediatric CDED/EEN follow-ups** — varies by study; I rounded or used ranges from my search results.

20. **Some of the older cited Gerhardt/Gupta/Borrelli/Zachos secondary data** — depend on what the aggregated reviews say vs primary source; slight discrepancies possible.

**When using this reference, Claude should**:
- Verify any PMID before citing it as a definitive source
- Phrase uncertain citations as "referenced in [context]" rather than asserting
- Use PubMed (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/) for definitive verification
- Update this document when primary source access reveals discrepancies
