# Acupuncture / Moxibustion

## THERAPY: acupuncture

### Evidence Grade
**[Weak-to-Moderate]** — one German RCT positive (CD), Chinese literature positive but methodologically limited.

### Mechanism (proposed)
- Vagal afferent stimulation → cholinergic anti-inflammatory pathway
- HPA axis modulation
- Local microcirculation effects (moxibustion — burning of *Artemisia vulgaris*/mugwort on or near acupoints, adds thermal component)
- Endogenous opioid release (nociceptive pain)

### Key Trials

**Joos S, et al. 2004** — *Scand J Gastroenterol* 39(8):762-9. **PMID: 15114043**. German RCT in active mild-moderate CD. n=51. **10 sessions of traditional acupuncture + moxibustion over 4 weeks** vs sham (superficial non-acupoint needling).
- CDAI decrease: 87 points active vs 39 points sham (p=0.003)
- Both groups improved (large placebo component)
- Quality of life improved more in active group
- Modest additional benefit beyond placebo effect

**Bao CH, et al. 2022** — *EClinicalMedicine* 45:101300. **PMID: 35198926**. Chinese RCT in mild-moderate CD. n=66. Acupuncture + moxibustion 3x/week × 12 weeks vs sham. Significant CDAI reduction, improved quality of life, beneficial shifts in microbiota, reduced inflammatory markers. Higher quality than older Chinese literature.

**Joos S, et al. 2006** (UC): similar positive RCT for active UC.

### Dose/Regimen
- Typical: 10-20 sessions over 4-8 weeks, then maintenance
- Find licensed acupuncturist (NCCAOM certified in US); ideally with IBD experience
- Single sessions $60-120 typical

### Safety
- Very safe with trained practitioner
- Local bruising, minor bleeding, rare infection (single-use needles standard)
- **Moxibustion burn risk** — watch skin
- Avoid needles through active cellulitis, edema, vascular malformations

### Drug Interactions
- None

### Practical Bottom Line
Reasonable adjunct. Probable mechanism includes stress reduction + vagal stimulation. German and Chinese evidence moderate. **Don't expect remission induction on its own for moderate-severe disease.** Low risk, substantial cost/time commitment.
