# Autoimmune Protocol (AIP)

## DIET: aip

### Evidence Grade
**[Anecdotal]** — no RCTs in CD.

### Background
- Paleo-based elimination of grains, legumes, dairy, nightshades, eggs, nuts/seeds, alcohol, coffee, processed foods, NSAIDs

### Evidence
- **Konijeti GG, et al. 2017** — Scripps: small IBD pilot (UC+CD), n=15, open-label. 73% achieved clinical remission by week 6. No control.
- Konijeti et al. 2020: 15/17 UC/CD patients improved on AIP
- Very restrictive; long-term adherence concerns; nutritional adequacy risk
- No RCT evidence

### Practical Bottom Line
Experimental, very restrictive, not well-supported by RCT evidence. **Work with an IBD-trained dietitian** — restrictive diets carry nutritional risk (especially post-resection with potential malabsorption of B12, fat-soluble vitamins, calcium, iron, zinc).
