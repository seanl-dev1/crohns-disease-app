# Low FODMAP Diet

## DIET: low-fodmap

### Evidence Grade
Evidence primarily for IBS; IBD patients with IBS-overlap symptoms may benefit.

### Overview
- Evidence primarily for IBS; IBD patients with IBS-overlap symptoms may benefit
- Not disease-modifying for IBD inflammation
- Short-term (2-6 week) elimination then structured reintroduction

### Practical Bottom Line
Only useful if IBS-overlap symptoms. **Work with an IBD-trained dietitian** — restrictive diets carry nutritional risk (especially post-resection with potential malabsorption of B12, fat-soluble vitamins, calcium, iron, zinc).
