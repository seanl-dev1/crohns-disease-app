# Mediterranean Diet

## DIET: mediterranean

### Evidence Grade
**[Moderate]** — emerging as reasonable first-line dietary approach.

### Evidence
- DINE-CD (Lewis 2021, PMID: 34052278): 43.5% remission in CD, comparable to SCD
- Strazzabosco et al. 2025 (*Gastroenterology*): prospective cohort of newly-diagnosed CD — multiomic benefits on inflammation markers and microbiome diversity
- Easier to maintain, cardiometabolic benefits

### Practical Bottom Line
**Best starting point** for most CD patients — well-tolerated, heart-healthy, evidence-based. Not superior to SCD per DINE-CD, but much easier to sustain. **Work with an IBD-trained dietitian** — especially post-resection with potential malabsorption of B12, fat-soluble vitamins, calcium, iron, zinc.
