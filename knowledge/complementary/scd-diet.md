# Specific Carbohydrate Diet (SCD)

## DIET: scd

### Evidence Grade
**[Weak-to-Moderate]** — uncontrolled case series positive; RCT shows comparable to Mediterranean (not superior); some pediatric RCT signal.

### Background
- Developed by Elaine Gottschall (*Breaking the Vicious Cycle*, 1994). Eliminates all grains, most dairy (except hard cheese, homemade yogurt fermented 24h), sugar, starchy vegetables, processed foods.
- **Rationale**: disaccharides/polysaccharides persist in inflamed intestine, feed dysbiotic flora

### Evidence
- **Suskind DL, et al. 2014/2018** (various PMIDs): pediatric case series showing remission
- **Suskind DL, et al. 2020** (PMID: 33291229): randomized pediatric trial, SCD vs modified SCD vs whole foods — all three induced remission
- **Lewis JD, et al. 2021 (DINE-CD)** — *Gastroenterology* 161(3):837-852. **PMID: 34052278**. Largest adult CD dietary RCT. n=194. SCD vs Mediterranean × 6 weeks.
  - Symptomatic remission: **46.5% SCD vs 43.5% MD** (NS; not superior)
  - Fecal calprotectin response: 34.8% SCD vs 30.8% MD (NS)
  - CRP response: 5.4% SCD vs 3.6% MD (NS)
  - **Conclusion: SCD NOT superior to Mediterranean diet. Mediterranean preferred due to ease and cardiovascular benefits.**

### Practical Bottom Line
Reasonable alternative, not superior to Mediterranean per DINE-CD, much harder to follow. **Work with an IBD-trained dietitian** — restrictive diets carry nutritional risk (especially post-resection with potential malabsorption of B12, fat-soluble vitamins, calcium, iron, zinc).
