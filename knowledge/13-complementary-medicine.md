# Complementary & Natural Medicine for Crohn's Disease — Evidence Reference

> **This file has been split.** See the individual per-topic files in the [`complementary/`](./complementary/) folder for grep-friendly lookup and per-topic maintenance.

> Framing: "What does the evidence actually say?" — no boosterism, no dismissiveness.
> Evidence grades: **[Strong]** = multiple well-powered RCTs; **[Moderate]** = at least one positive RCT or consistent supporting data; **[Weak]** = small/inconsistent trials, mechanistic only, or negative RCTs that don't rule out niche benefit; **[Anecdotal]** = case reports, uncontrolled series, preclinical only.
> Most IBD complementary evidence is for **ulcerative colitis (UC)**. CD-specific evidence is thinner. Flag this when extrapolating.

---

*This document is a reference aid, not medical advice. Crohn's disease management requires individualized care with a qualified gastroenterologist. Most "natural" interventions are adjunctive — they work alongside, not instead of, evidence-based medical therapy when disease is active. The most dangerous supplement is the one that delays effective treatment while inflammation silently damages the bowel.*
