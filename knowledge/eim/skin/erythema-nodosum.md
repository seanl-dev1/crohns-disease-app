# Erythema Nodosum (EN)

Reference file for Claude. Technical language. Evidence graded where available.

## EIM: Erythema Nodosum

- **Prevalence**: **4-15%** in CD (higher than UC); F:M ~3-4:1.
- **Appearance**: Tender, warm, 1-5 cm erythematous-to-violaceous nodules; **anterior shins most common**, also forearms, thighs.
- **Course**: **Parallels gut activity**; self-resolving over 2-4 weeks leaving "bruise-like" discoloration (no ulceration or scarring).
- **Workup**: Clinical diagnosis; biopsy only if atypical (shows septal panniculitis without vasculitis).
- **Treatment**: Treat the underlying IBD flare (steroids, 5-ASA, biologics). Compression, leg elevation, NSAIDs (cautiously). Anti-TNF (adalimumab, infliximab) reported effective in refractory cases.

### Treatment Principle
Erythema nodosum typically responds to gut-targeted therapy — activity tracks with bowel inflammation; treat the gut.
