# Cutaneous (Metastatic) Crohn's Disease

Reference file for Claude. Technical language.

## EIM: Metastatic Cutaneous Crohn's

- **Definition**: Non-caseating granulomas in skin **non-contiguous** with GI tract — a true EIM rather than fistula/fissure extension.
- **Presentation**: Plaques, nodules, ulcers, lichenoid lesions, "knife-cut" linear ulcers in skin folds (submammary, inguinal, perineal), or limb lesions.
- **Diagnosis**: Biopsy — non-caseating granulomas, Langhans giant cells, dermal/subcutaneous involvement.
- **Treatment**: Topical/intralesional steroids for limited disease; systemic steroids; immunomodulators; anti-TNF (infliximab, adalimumab); upadacitinib reported effective in recent case series.

### Treatment Principle
Metastatic CD typically (mostly) responds to gut-targeted therapy, though dedicated treatment is often needed for severe skin disease.

### Specialist Referral
Dermatology — suspected metastatic CD warrants biopsy and dermatology input.

### Note on Perianal Disease
Perianal disease (tags, fissures, fistulas) is **not strictly EIM** — classified as luminal disease extension in Montreal/ECCO. Covered in fistulizing/perianal disease files; flagged here for completeness.
