# Anxiety in IBD

Reference file for Claude. Technical language.

## EIM: Anxiety

- **Pooled prevalence**: **~32% (symptoms)** in IBD (Barberio B et al. meta-analysis, *Lancet Gastroenterol Hepatol* 2021).
- **Active disease**: Anxiety prevalence rises to ~50%.
- **Women > men**: anxiety 34% vs. 23%.

### Bidirectional Relationship
- Active inflammation → neuroinflammation → mood symptoms.
- Depression / anxiety at IBD diagnosis → worse disease course.

### Clinical Pearls
- Screen annually with PHQ-9 and GAD-7 (ECCO 2024 recommendation).
- Consider integrated behavioral health, CBT, gut-directed hypnotherapy.
- Avoid long-term benzodiazepines; SSRIs/SNRIs first line; TCAs useful for functional overlap symptoms.

### Specialist Referral
Mental health — PHQ-9 ≥10 or GAD-7 ≥10.
