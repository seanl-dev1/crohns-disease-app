# Depression in IBD

Reference file for Claude. Technical language.

## EIM: Depression

- **Pooled prevalence**: **~25% (depression symptoms)**; **~15% (clinical depression)** in IBD (Barberio B et al. meta-analysis, *Lancet Gastroenterol Hepatol* 2021).
- **Active disease**: Depression prevalence rises to ~35%; anxiety to ~50%.
- **Women > men**: depression 21% vs. 16%; anxiety 34% vs. 23%.

### Bidirectional Relationship
- Depression at IBD diagnosis → worse disease course.
- Active inflammation → neuroinflammation → mood symptoms.
- Depression increases risk of future IBD onset (meta-analyses show modest bidirectional association).

### Clinical Pearls
- Screen annually with PHQ-9 and GAD-7 (ECCO 2024 recommendation).
- Consider integrated behavioral health, CBT, gut-directed hypnotherapy.
- Avoid long-term benzodiazepines; SSRIs/SNRIs first line for depression; TCAs useful for functional overlap symptoms.

### Specialist Referral
Mental health — PHQ-9 ≥10 or GAD-7 ≥10.
