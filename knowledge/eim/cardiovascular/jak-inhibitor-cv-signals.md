# JAK Inhibitors and Cardiovascular Safety Signals

Reference file for Claude. Technical language. Evidence graded where available.

## EIM: JAK Inhibitor CV Signals

- **JAK inhibitors (tofacitinib, upadacitinib)**:
  - Boxed warning for MACE and VTE based on ORAL Surveillance trial (tofacitinib vs anti-TNF in RA >50yo with ≥1 CV risk factor).
  - **IBD data less concerning**: 2025 network meta-analysis of 26 IBD RCTs (10,537 patients) showed **no significant increase in MACE or VTE** overall. Dose-dependent trends: tofacitinib 5 mg BID trend toward MACE; upadacitinib 30 mg QD trend toward VTE/CVE.
  - Clinical approach: risk-stratify (age >65, prior CV/VTE, smoking); prefer JAK in lower-risk patients or when other options exhausted.

### Evidence Grade
- JAK inhibitors in IBD do not increase MACE/VTE overall — **Moderate** (2025 network MA in IBD; RA class warning exists).
