# Non-Melanoma Skin Cancer (NMSC)

Reference file for Claude. Technical language.

## EIM: Non-Melanoma Skin Cancer

- **Non-melanoma skin cancer (NMSC)**: **Increased with thiopurines** (relative risk ~2-6×); cumulative and duration-dependent; may persist after drug discontinuation.
- **Prevention**: Broad-spectrum sunscreen daily, sun avoidance, **annual full-body skin exam** — especially if on thiopurines or anti-TNF.

### Monitoring
- **Annual full-body skin exam**: Especially on thiopurines or anti-TNF.
