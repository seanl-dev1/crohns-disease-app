# Episcleritis

Reference file for Claude. Technical language.

## EIM: Episcleritis

- **Most common ocular EIM**.
- **Appearance**: Painless or mildly uncomfortable red eye; no vision change; sectoral or diffuse.
- **Course**: **Parallels gut activity**.
- **Treatment**: Cool compresses, topical NSAIDs or lubricants; often self-limiting; treat gut.
- Can be distinguished from scleritis by phenylephrine test (blanches the episcleral vessels).

### Treatment Principle
Episcleritis typically responds to gut-targeted therapy — activity tracks with bowel inflammation; treat the gut.

### Overall Ocular EIM Note
Overall ocular EIMs: **4-12%** of IBD; CD > UC. Annual eye exam recommended by ECCO 2024, more frequent if symptoms or history.
