# Exercise with an Ostomy (Crohn's/IBD)

**Scope:** Parastomal hernia risk/epidemiology, inactivity data, staged prevention programming (first 3 months / months 3–6 / 6+), support belt evidence, swimming and contact-sport considerations, running with an ostomy.

**Evidence grading shorthand:**
- **[A]** — Multiple RCTs / systematic review of RCTs, consistent findings
- **[B]** — At least one RCT or several well-designed non-randomized studies
- **[C]** — Small uncontrolled studies, case series, or extrapolation from adjacent fields
- **[D]** — Expert opinion / mechanistic reasoning only

## 13. Exercise with an Ostomy

### 13.1 Parastomal hernia — the key risk

- **Incidence:** Cumulative risk ~50% by 5 years post-stoma. By stoma type: urostomy ~27%, colostomy ~40%, ileostomy ~24% (range by study).
- **Inactivity association:** Statistically significant inverse relationship — median 579 MET-min/week with hernia vs 1689 MET-min/week without (North et al., *WOC Nurs*, PMID 35714337).

### 13.2 Prevention programming

**First 3 months post-stoma:**
- No lifting >4.5–6.8 kg (10–15 lb)
- No direct abdominal resistance (crunches, planks, sit-ups)
- Walking encouraged progressively

**Months 3–6:**
- Graded core retraining — diaphragmatic breathing → transverse abdominis activation → dead bug → bird dog → anti-rotation (Pallof)
- Avoid Valsalva
- Introduce light resistance training with stoma support belt

**Month 6+:**
- Full resistance training possible, with continued attention to technique
- Running, swimming, cycling all compatible
- Avoid pure crunch/sit-up patterns lifelong — anti-extension/rotation core preferable anyway

### 13.3 Support belts

- **Purpose:** Provide gentle circumferential compression around the stoma, reducing outward bulging during intra-abdominal pressure spikes
- **Evidence [C]:** Observational data suggest ~78% reduction in parastomal hernia risk with consistent belt use during activity; RCT evidence limited
- **Products:** Coloplast Brava, Stealthbelt, Salts Comfizz, nu-Hope. Sizing/fit matters — should sit level with stoma without pinching
- **When to use:** All resistance training, running, lifting anything >10 kg outside gym, contact sports

### 13.4 Swimming

- Ostomy pouches are water-resistant when seal is secure
- Empty pouch before entering water
- Apply wafer ≥1 h before swim for maximum adhesion
- Water-resistant tape strips around flange edge reduce lift/leak
- Mini/stealth pouches available for more discretion

### 13.5 Contact sports

- Protective guards: Stealthbelt X, Ostomy Armor, custom plastic shields
- Avoid in first 6 months post-stoma
- Rugby, American football, martial arts, wrestling — discuss explicitly with surgeon/stoma nurse
- Non-contact alternatives (tennis, golf, track) carry minimal stoma-specific risk

### 13.6 Running specifically

- No contraindication once fully healed (~3 months)
- Long runs: stoma output management (small, frequent ORS sips); pouch emptying every 30–60 min on longer efforts
- Public examples: Adele Roberts (first woman with stoma to run all World Marathon Majors)
