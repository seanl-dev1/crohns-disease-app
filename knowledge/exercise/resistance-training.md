# Resistance Training Specifics for Crohn's

**Scope:** Protein needs (ESPEN IBD + athlete extrapolation), whey + leucine + vitamin D sarcopenia data, bone-loading principles, programming for major muscle groups, and Valsalva/hernia considerations during lifting.

**Evidence grading shorthand:**
- **[A]** — Multiple RCTs / systematic review of RCTs, consistent findings
- **[B]** — At least one RCT or several well-designed non-randomized studies
- **[C]** — Small uncontrolled studies, case series, or extrapolation from adjacent fields
- **[D]** — Expert opinion / mechanistic reasoning only

## 7. Resistance Training Specifics

### 7.1 Protein needs

- **ESPEN IBD guideline** (Bischoff et al., *Clin Nutr*, 2023; PMID 36739756; original 2017 guideline PMID 28131521):
  - **Active disease:** 1.2–1.5 g/kg/day
  - **Remission:** ~1.0 g/kg/day (as general population)
- **For active resistance-training IBD patients:** evidence-informed target of **1.2–1.6 g/kg/day**, potentially up to **1.8–2.0 g/kg/day** post-operatively or during recomposition phases (extrapolation from ISSN / ACSM sports nutrition stands). **[C]**
- **Whey protein + leucine + vitamin D + resistance training** — significant benefit in sarcopenia (PROVIDE trial, *J Am Med Dir Assoc*; and systematic reviews PMID 36771225, 32928579). Whey has anti-inflammatory and intestinal-barrier-supporting properties that may benefit IBD specifically. **[B]** for sarcopenia generally; **[C]** for IBD-specific extrapolation.
- Spread protein across 3–4 meals, ~0.3–0.4 g/kg per meal, for optimal muscle protein synthesis pulses.

### 7.2 Bone loading for osteoporosis prevention

- **Resistance + impact combined** (Robinson et al., *Gastroenterology*, 1998; PMID 9649456): progressive low-impact exercise over 12 months in 117 Crohn's patients → significant BMD gain at greater trochanter (mean difference 4.67 g/cm², 95% CI 0.86–8.48; P=0.02). Hip/spine non-significant but trending.
- **Jones et al. RCT** (*APT*, 2020; PMID 33119156): 6 months combined impact + resistance → improved **lumbar spine BMD**.
- **Mechanical loading principles** (Frost's mechanostat):
  - Strain magnitude > habitual loading is required
  - Impact (jumps, hops, running) drives greater osteogenic response than isometric loading
  - Few, high-strain cycles outperform many low-strain cycles
  - Variety of directions/patterns superior to single-vector loading
- **Practical osteogenic programming for IBD:**
  - Resistance: squats, deadlifts, step-ups, overhead press, rows — 2–3x/week
  - Impact: jump rope, box jumps, hop variations, plyometric step-ups — 2x/week, short sessions (5–10 min), start 10–20 foot contacts and build gradually
  - Modify impact based on pelvic floor status and knee/hip joint health

### 7.3 Programming — major muscle groups, 2–3x/week

**Minimum effective dose** (Androulakis-Korakakis et al., PMC 11127831) — 1 set of resistance per muscle group per week produces measurable strength gains. Reasonable IBD starting point.

**Intermediate target:**
- Lower body (quads, hamstrings, glutes, calves): 2x/week
- Upper body push (chest, shoulders, triceps): 2x/week
- Upper body pull (back, biceps): 2x/week
- Core anti-rotation/anti-extension: 2–3x/week

**Sample 3-day split (remission, intermediate):**
- **Day A:** Goblet squat, Romanian deadlift, dumbbell bench press, row, carry variation
- **Day B:** Step-up, hip thrust, overhead press, lat pulldown, anti-rotation (Pallof press)
- **Day C:** Deadlift (moderate), split squat, incline press, face pull, farmer carry

### 7.4 Avoiding Valsalva with abdominal hernia concerns

- **Valsalva maneuver** (forceful expiration against closed glottis) spikes intra-abdominal pressure to 100–300 mmHg — useful for heavy lifting to stabilize spine, but increases hernia risk at surgical sites and with stoma.
- **Post-resection / stoma patients:**
  - Exhale through exertion phase rather than breath-hold
  - Avoid 1RM testing
  - Cap loads at ~80% 1RM or ~RPE 8 (2 reps in reserve)
  - Consider abdominal binder or stoma support belt during compound lifts (see exercise-with-ostomy.md)
- **Core training without Valsalva:** dead bug, bird dog, Pallof press, suitcase carry, side plank (modified) — favor anti-movement over movement-through-range.
