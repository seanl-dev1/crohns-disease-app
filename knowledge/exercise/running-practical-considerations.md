## 6. Running and Crohn's — Practical Considerations

### 6.1 Runner's diarrhea — mechanism

Two pathways (de Oliveira et al., *Sports Med*, 2014; Costa et al. frameworks):

1. **Circulatory–gastrointestinal pathway.** At ~70% VO₂max, splanchnic blood flow decreases up to 80%. Mucosal hypoxia → ATP depletion → tight junction disruption → increased intestinal permeability → LPS translocation → systemic inflammatory response. Prolonged: mucosal ischemia, exercise-induced colitis.
2. **Neuroendocrine–gastrointestinal pathway.** Sympathetic drive during exercise accelerates colonic motility, reduces absorption, and can precipitate urgency.

**Prevalence:** 30–90% of endurance runners report GI symptoms. Likely higher in IBD runners (no direct comparative trials).

Reference: Pugh et al. *Med Sci Sports Exerc* (PMC 5694518) — intestinal permeability and GI function markers in symptomatic vs asymptomatic runners.

### 6.2 Dehydration and electrolytes

- Sweat losses of 500–1500 mL/h; sodium ~0.5–2.0 g/L sweat.
- IBD patients, especially post–ileal resection, may have baseline volume/sodium depletion.
- See hydration-electrolytes.md for detailed rehydration guidance.

### 6.3 Timing meals around runs

Pragmatic strategy (extrapolated from sports nutrition + IBD practice):
- **3–4 h pre-run:** full meal, low-fat, low-residue, moderate protein.
- **1–2 h pre-run:** small, low-fiber, low-FODMAP snack (banana, white toast with almond butter, rice cake).
- **<60 min pre-run:** sip only — water or dilute electrolyte.
- **Avoid pre-run:** high-fat meals (delay gastric emptying), high-fiber (osmotic draw, colonic fermentation), sugar alcohols, caffeine if prokinetic for you, dairy if lactose-intolerant.

### 6.4 Bathroom and route planning

- Map toilets along routes (iOS/Android toilet apps; out-and-back near home/park with facilities).
- Carry a go-bag (wipes, change of shorts, plastic bag) for longer runs.
- Loperamide (2–4 mg, 30–60 min pre-run) is commonly used prophylactically by IBD runners in remission — should be cleared with GI. Not appropriate in active flare or in unexplained diarrhea.
- Avoid dehydration-induced ischemia by pre-hydrating and sipping during runs.

### 6.5 Fiber before exercise

- **Pre-run:** Lower residue, lower FODMAP in the 12–24 h before a long/hard run.
- **Non-exercise days / off-season:** Restore usual fiber intake for microbiome benefits.
- In Crohn's with any known stricture: cumulative fiber modulation is also a stricture/obstruction-risk tool (see contraindications.md).
