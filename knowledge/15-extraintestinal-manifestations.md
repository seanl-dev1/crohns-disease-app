## Overview

- **Lifetime prevalence**: up to **50%** of IBD patients develop ≥1 EIM.
- **Cross-sectional pooled prevalence**: 24% overall, **35% in CD** (Rogler 2024).
- **ECCO 2024 framework** (Gordon H, et al. *J Crohn's Colitis* 2024;18(1):1-37): Classical, Associations, Complications, Treatment-related.
- **Legacy framework**: reactive/gut-parallel vs. independent/gut-discordant.

> **Pearl**: If the EIM parallels the gut, treating the gut usually treats the EIM. If the EIM is independent, you likely need a biologic with dual indication (anti-TNF) or a separate therapy in co-management.
