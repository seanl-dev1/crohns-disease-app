So which do you actually use?

You don't have to pick one. A mature brand system has a master mark plus secondary expressions for different contexts. Here's a rough map of which variation fits which moment.

  • App iconRefined master or Layered Leaf — the workhorses, solid at any size.
  • Splash screenBerry Leaf or Negative-Space C — a beat of discovery in the first two seconds.
  • Marketing heroHeart Leaf or Mirrored Pair — warmth that a 1:1 app icon can't carry.
  • Print / swagFramed Leaf or Sprig — emblematic, stamp-like, repeats beautifully.
  • Editorial / blogWillow or Almond — slender, reads like a literary flourish.
